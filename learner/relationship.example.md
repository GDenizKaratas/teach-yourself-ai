# Relationship (who they are to you, not how they learn)

`learner/profile.md` models how this person learns. This file models **how to be with them**: what to call them, what they decided, what is going on in their life, what has felt good or wrong. `tools/learn status` prints the frontmatter and the dated lines at every session start, so none of it depends on memory.

Frontmatter keys (plain `key: value`, no quotes):
- `name` — their name. `address_as` — how they want to be addressed ("Ayşe", "hocam", a nickname, "sen"). Asked at onboarding ("Sana nasıl sesleneyim?").
- `closing` — the goodbye they like ("yarın görüşürüz", "görüşürüz", none). Use it only when the next session is actually planned (see `rhythm`); otherwise name the real next time.
- `tone` — `direct-critical` | `warm` | `neutral`. Asked at onboarding ("Nasıl bir öğretmen istersin?"). Overrides the general voice in `CLAUDE.md`.
- `humour` — `none` | `light`. `praise` — `evidence-only` | `occasional`.
- `name_frequency` — `open+close` (default: the name at the opening, at the closing, and on one important verdict) | `rare`.
- `rhythm` — when they usually study; the wrap-up derives the closing from it.
